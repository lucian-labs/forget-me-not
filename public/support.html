Common questions

How is this different from a to-do list?

A to-do list assumes things get finished. Forget Me Not is for the things that come back — laundry, water, stretching, dishes. Each reminder fills up over time, and when you mark it done its timer starts again.

What do the colours mean?

Each reminder has a bar that fills as its time runs out: green when you're fine, orange when it's getting close, red when it's overdue. It's meant to be glanceable — you shouldn't have to read anything to know what needs you.

Why did a reminder come back sooner (or later) than I set?

That's deliberate. Timings are nudged slightly each cycle so reminders don't all pile up at exactly the same moment every day.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Your reminders stay on your device, and sync between your own devices through your iCloud account if you're signed in.

How do I move my reminders to another device?

On the iPhone, iPad and Mac app, signing in to the same iCloud account keeps everything in step automatically. In the web app, use Settings → Share to send everything across with a QR code or a link.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for Forget Me Not in your device's Settings. In the web app, notifications need to be switched on in the app's own Settings as well — and on iPhone, the web app has to be added to your Home Screen first before the browser will allow them.

How do I delete everything?

Settings has a clear-data option, or just delete the app. There's no account left behind anywhere — see the privacy policy.
