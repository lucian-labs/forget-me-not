Privacy Policy

Forget Me Not · last updated 13 August 2026

The short version: there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your reminders are yours. I never see them.

What the app stores

Forget Me Not keeps your reminders, notes, categories and settings on the device you're using. There is no sign-up and no user account, so nothing is tied to your identity.

The iPhone, iPad and Mac app

  • Your data is stored on your device.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, it syncs through your own private iCloud account so your devices stay in step. That data lives in your iCloud, governed by Apple's privacy policy. I have no access to it.
  • Reminder text you write is used on-device to generate a small icon and the occasional suggestion. This processing happens on the device using Apple's built-in system features.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally by the app. Nothing is sent to me.

The web app (tasks.lucianlabs.ca)

  • Everything is stored in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your browser — with one exception, below.
  • If you switch on notifications, your browser's anonymous push subscription, plus the title of each active reminder and the time it's next due, are stored on a server I run (push.lucianlabs.ca) so it can send the notification at the right moment. That's the only reason it's stored, it isn't shared with anyone, and it is never used to build a profile.
  • Switching notifications off deletes that subscription and its reminder titles from the server. Reminders you complete or delete are removed from it as the app syncs.
  • If you don't turn on notifications, nothing about your reminders ever reaches a server.

What I don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no ad identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is nobody to sell it to and nothing to sell.
  • No third-party trackers embedded in the apps.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or use Settings to clear your data, and it's gone. For the web app, turning notifications off removes what the notification server holds; clearing your browser data removes the rest. Because there are no accounts, there's no server-side profile to request or erase.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
